# The playable loop

1. Open landing and select Start exploring.
2. Enter a valid-looking student ID. Check the derived student email.
3. Enter the campus at (640, 915), within Main Gate interaction range.
4. Move with WASD/arrows or the D-pad. Solid building footprints stop movement.
5. Press E / Investigate near a marker. The world pauses while the dialog is open.
6. Finish Welcome to EastQuest. Receive Explorer Pass and 50 XP.
7. Move north to y≈650 and west to the Library (330,650).
8. Answer the research question. Wrong choices allow retries without granting XP.
9. Explore the other locations at (950,650), (330,325), (950,325).
10. Five keys and 450 XP trigger EWU EXPLORER. Continue exploring or inspect collection.
11. Reload: saved progress remains; the avatar starts at Main Gate.
12. Profile → Prototype settings → confirmed reset starts a fresh run.

A closed ACTIVE quest remains resumable. A completed quest can be revisited but never
grants a second reward. Locked quests explain that the tutorial comes first.
Knowledge questions are general education, not official EWU policy.
The support scenario covers respectful listening and resource awareness, not medical procedures.
