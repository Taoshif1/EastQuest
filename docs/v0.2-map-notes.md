## Ground priority

Main Outer Gate → security scanner → raised entry → punch gates → Admin
circulation → courtyard. The sequence is FIELD_OBSERVED; exact gate/scanner
positions are APPROXIMATED. The courtyard and surrounding block relationship
follow the drawings and directory. The central north circulation opening and
clearance around wing lightwells were corrected after connectivity checks.
The Admin/D opening was widened to join its unobstructed lobby gap.

## Known limits

The map is a rectified architectural interpretation. It does not preserve every
sloping exterior line, angular gallery wall, measured corridor width or doorway.
Fan seating is original procedural artwork; it is not exact seating capacity.
Library and laboratory furnishings, office subdivisions, stairs and lift offsets
require field verification. The auditorium on upper floors is simplified.
FUB exterior is context; its nine upper floors and interior are not implemented.
Roof access and individual lift stops are not verified. No GPS accuracy is claimed.

See campus-source-register.md for evidence and ewu-campus-survey-template.md for
the next physical checks. See v0.2-validation.md for executed QA and release state.
